# CRT television profiles

These profiles model a home CRT television playback chain by broad era:

- `crt-tv-1970s.txt`
- `crt-tv-1980s.txt`
- `crt-tv-1990s.txt`
- `crt-tv-2000s.txt`

They are intentionally organized by era rather than pretending that one EQ curve represents every model.

The hardware research documents what can actually be supported. The tonal curves remain experimental where no calibrated acoustic response is available.

Do not normally stack a CRT-TV profile with an arcade cabinet profile: they represent alternative playback chains.
