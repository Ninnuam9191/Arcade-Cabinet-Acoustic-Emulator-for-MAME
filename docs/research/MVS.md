# MVS Noise Notes

## Why noise is included in the research model

Reports from MVS owners describe audible continuous hiss/noise on some boards,
and repair logs show that the analog power-amplifier/output stage can materially
affect the speaker output. This is useful evidence for the emulator, but it does
not establish one universal MVS noise floor.

### Do not treat this as an MVS IR

Noise is not part of a linear impulse response in the useful sense required by
a convolution model. A measured cabinet IR should represent the linear acoustic
transfer function; additive noise should remain a separate optional process.

### Candidate noise categories

- broadband amplifier hiss
- PSU/ground-related hum or buzz
- digital switching contamination
- amplifier clipping/distortion
- speaker/mechanical noise

These have different spectra and should not be replaced with generic white noise.

## Current project status

No additive MVS noise generator is enabled in the supplied presets. Equalizer APO
is being used here primarily for deterministic filtering/convolution; an external
VST/noise stage can be added later when a repeatable measurement exists.

## Useful evidence

- MVS reports describe continuous hiss on some cabinets/boards.
- Japanese MV-4F repair/review material discusses the MVS volume control, speaker
  outputs and distortion at higher settings.
- MVS hardware documentation shows different amplifier implementations across
  board revisions, so noise/distortion should be treated as revision-dependent.
